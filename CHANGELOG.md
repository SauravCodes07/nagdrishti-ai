# Changelog — NagDrishti AI

All notable changes and milestones for **NagDrishti AI** are documented below.

---

## [1.0.0] - Production Release

### 🌧️ Crisis Intelligence & GIS Mapping
- **Nagpur Crisis Command Center**: Real-time KPI monitoring for all 10 NMC administrative zones.
- **Interactive Multi-layer GIS Crisis Map**: Waterlogging, rainfall radar, road damage, traffic density, and emergency resource markers.
- **Safe Route Planner (`/safe-route`)**: Submersion-aware routing engine calculating optimal bypass routes with safety scores and live ETAs.
- **AI Rainfall & Waterlogging Predictions (`/ai-predictions`)**: Predictive risk modeling with zonal advisory outputs.

### 🚨 Civic & Emergency Response
- **Alerts & Broadcast Notifications (`/alerts`)**: Multi-channel warning dispatch for severe waterlogging and bridge closures.
- **Citizen Hazard Reporting (`/citizen-reports`)**: Crowd-sourced issue reporting with image verification and municipal resolution workflow.
- **Emergency Response & Resource Tracker (`/emergency-response` & `/resources`)**: High-capacity dewatering pump tracking, NDRF flood rescue teams, and vehicle dispatching.
- **Heavy Rainfall 8-Stage Simulator**: Interactive demo simulator demonstrating real-time crisis escalation and automated municipal response.

### 🎨 Design & Mobile-First Experience
- Official NagDrishti AI branding, logo integration, and browser favicon.
- Bhagwa/Saffron (`#FF7722`) + Deep Navy (`#0B192C`) + Warm White civic design system.
- Mobile bottom navigation bar with responsive drawer.
- Production Vercel build compatibility with single-page application routing.
